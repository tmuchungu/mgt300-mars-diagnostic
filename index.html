The MARS Diagnostic Panel

Four independent readings feed into one outcome: individual behavior and performance.

MOTIVATION

The forces within a person that affect the direction, intensity, and persistence of voluntary behavior.

ABILITY

Natural aptitudes, learned capabilities, and habits required to successfully complete a task.

ROLE PERCEPTIONS

The extent to which people understand the job duties (roles) assigned to or expected of them.

SITUATIONAL FACTORS

Conditions beyond the employee's immediate control that constrain, facilitate, or cue behavior and performance.
